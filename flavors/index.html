Flavor Rankings

Top Ramen | Flavor Rankings

Number 1

Chicken

Chicken is the most popular flavor of Top Ramen, and for good reason. It has a comforting, nostalgic, and reliable savory broth that works well on its own or upgraded with add-ons.

Number 2

Soy Sauce

Soy Sauce is a distinct flavor that adds a salty-sweet umami punch to Top Ramen, making it more complex than plain chicken.

Number 3

Beef

Beef is a hearty and robust flavor, though some find the artificial beef seasoning a bit too salty or heavy, dropping it down third place.

Number 4

Chili

Chili is a fiery flavor that adds a nice spicy kick for instant noodles, but the spicy powder can be harsh if not mixed right.

Number 5

Shrimp

Shrimp is a bottom-tier flavor for most casual eaters, lacking a true shellfish depth and mostly tasting like fishy salt water.
