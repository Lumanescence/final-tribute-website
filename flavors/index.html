How Much These Flavors Are Loved

Rank Flavor Taste Like
1 (most favorite) Chicken a comforting, nostalgic, and reliable savory broth that works well on its own or upgraded with add-ons
2 Soy Sauce a distinct salty-sweet umami punch that's a bit more complex than plain chicken
3 Beef a hearty and robust flaver, though some find the artificial beef seasoning a bit too salty or heavy
4 Chili a nice spicy kick for instant noodles, but the spicy powder can be harsh if not mixed right
5 (least favorite) Shrimp bottom-tier for most casual eaters, lacks a true shellfish depth and mostly tastes like fishy salt water
