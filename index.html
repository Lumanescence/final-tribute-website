What Is Top Ramen?

Top Ramen is an instant noodle brand under Nissin founded by Momofuku Ando in Japan. He invented the instant noodle and decided to share it with the world.

In 1972, Top Ramen began producing their instant noodles in the US. This was the first mass-marketed instant ramen manufactured and sold in the country.

How To Make Top Ramen

Top Ramen is very easy to make. All you need is a pot, water, and the ramen.

First, boil 2 cups of water in a pot. Then, add the ramen noodles and cook for 3 minutes. Finally, add the seasoning packet and stir well.
